\noindent\textbf{Composite SUS Formula \& UAT Operational Scenarios ($N \ge 10$ Evaluators):} $\mathrm{SUS} = 2.5 \times \sum_{i=1}^{10} c_i \in [0, 100]$ (Industry acceptability threshold $\ge 68.0$; Excellent usability $\ge 80.3$). Evaluators execute four timed tasks prior to scoring: \textbf{Scenario A} (switch CCTV feed and verify active helmet color counts within 10 s); \textbf{Scenario B} (hot-swap the GDUT 5-class checkpoint via \nolinkurl{/api/switch_model} and verify \texttt{is\_helmet\_only=true}, String-First 4-color helmet distribution, and real-time \texttt{none} violation alerting---noting that \textit{Solo Focus} activates only when hot-swapped to a \texttt{person}-class model); \textbf{Scenario C} (inspect a forensic violation snapshot and log HITL feedback via \texttt{Confirm}/\texttt{False Positive}/\texttt{Edit}); and \textbf{Scenario D} (upload a site photograph in \textit{Image Audit} and verify bounding boxes and inference latency).
