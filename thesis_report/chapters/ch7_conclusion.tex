\section{Summary of Findings}

This work delivered a rigorously audited, end-to-end safety helmet and color classification framework spanning an auxiliary cross-domain two-class benchmark (\textbf{Proposed V1} on \texttt{SHWD} and zero-shot \texttt{HHW}) and a primary deduplicated five-class industrial benchmark (\textbf{Proposed V2} on \texttt{GDUT-HWD}), operationalized inside the containerized \textbf{AEGIS-CCTV Enterprise VMS v3.0} platform. Across our empirical and engineering campaigns, all five project objectives (\textbf{O1--O5}) and four research questions (\textbf{RQ1--RQ4}) were systematically resolved with verifiable artifacts:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Leakage-Free, Color-Safe Five-Class Data Pipeline:} Auditing all 3,174 raw Pascal VOC images of \texttt{GDUT-HWD} via RGB pixel SHA-256 hashing uncovered and purged six exact cross-split duplicates from \texttt{trainval} while retaining all 1,587 official test images intact (yielding 3,168 clean images and 18,849 bounding boxes across \texttt{blue}, \texttt{white}, \texttt{yellow}, \texttt{red}, and \texttt{none}). Disabling hue/saturation distortion (\texttt{hsv\_h = 0}, \texttt{hsv\_s = 0}) preserved four-color helmet fidelity while offline hard augmentation doubled Stage 2 training coverage to 2,530 images.
  \item \textbf{Unified-Protocol Comparison Against Published SOTA Baselines (\texttt{B1} and \texttt{B2}):} Evaluated under the identical cryptographic \texttt{ce2a883e} dataset split and 48 matched Stage 2 hyperparameters across all 15 public Kaggle notebooks (Appendix~\ref{app:kaggle_catalog}, \texttt{75/75 MATCH}), \textbf{V2 \texttt{A6 (YOLO11s)}} achieves \textbf{88.94\% mAP@50} and \textbf{58.08\% mAP@50--95} (\textbf{87.08\% macro F1}, \textbf{9,476,783} fused deployed parameters, \textbf{15.207 ms} Tesla T4 FP32 median), outperforming \textbf{B1} (\texttt{YOLO11n-DCRCF}: \texttt{84.85\% / 54.77\%}, $+4.09 / +3.31$ pp gain; \texttt{B1} suffers $26.22\%$ transfer discontinuity and drops $-7.85$ pp vs. two-class SHWD paper claims) and \textbf{B2} (\texttt{YOLOv8s-DWR-ASPP-NWD}: \texttt{87.51\% / 56.59\%}, $+1.43 / +1.49$ pp gain at $-41.8\%$ fewer deployed parameters and $-32.1\%$ lower latency). Even \textbf{V2 \texttt{A6 (YOLOv8s)}} (\texttt{88.37\% / 57.37\%}) surpasses both published baselines.
  \item \textbf{Transparent Dual-Backbone Ablations (\texttt{A0--A6}) and Proposed V1 Cross-Domain Evidence:} Configuration \textbf{\texttt{A1}} (color-safe HardAug + \texttt{mixup = 0.1}) achieves the highest overall internal accuracy on five-class GDUT-HWD (\texttt{YOLO11s}: $93.40\%$ val / $89.76\%$ test mAP@50, $61.69\%$ val / $59.08\%$ test mAP@50--95), with pre-finetune initialization audits isolating the $-5.49$ pp \texttt{C2PSA} replacement penalty on \texttt{YOLO11s} \texttt{A5/A6} whereas \texttt{YOLOv8s A6} preserves $91.88\%$ initialization and reaches $93.21\%$ validation precision. In the isolated three-seed \textbf{Proposed V1} study (\texttt{SHWD\_V1/}), adding 12 PAN \texttt{RepConv} blocks (\texttt{V1 A2}) accelerates T4 FP16 inference to \textbf{13.55 $\pm$ 0.23 ms} ($73.8$ FPS, $p = 0.0386$) and delivers peak zero-shot \texttt{HHW} transfer (\textbf{95.78 $\pm$ 0.07\%} mAP@50, \textbf{56.48 $\pm$ 0.42\%} mAP@50--95, $4.7\times$ lower variance).
  \item \textbf{Verified Edge Optimization and Industrial VMS Delivery:} Hardware profiling on RTX 3050 confirmed that vectorized \texttt{torch.gather} routing, verified \nolinkurl{RepConv.switch_to_deploy()} branch fusion ($-300,992$ params before BN fold, $-10,800$ affine params during \texttt{model.fuse()}, $-6.3\%$ AMP FP16 latency, $6.28 \times 10^{-6}$ mean FP32 deviation), CUDA AMP FP16, and micro-batching ($N=4$) accelerate \texttt{Rep-YOLO11s-GDUT-A6} from 73.4 FPS ($13.62$ ms) to \textbf{118.2 FPS} ($8.46$ ms/frame, $1.61\times$ GPU speedup, $\Delta = 0$ boxes), while within-frame paired MobileNetV3 Re-ID adds only $+0.16$ ms amortized overhead ($15.33$ ms, 65.2 FPS). Packaged as a 6-microservice Enterprise Docker stack (including four-table PostgreSQL 16 persistence via \nolinkurl{src/db/database.py}, Linux cgroup Prometheus/Grafana observability, Class-Agnostic NMS, String-First color resolution, Binary WebSocket \texttt{/ws/webcam}, and CI/CD), \textbf{AEGIS-CCTV v3.0} passed \textbf{139/139 CPU \texttt{pytest}} cases (\textbf{82 passed, 1 skipped} in Clean-Room CI) and sustained \textbf{446.7--501.1 RPS} in-process and \textbf{225.2--260.8 RPS} across 6 Docker containers at $0.0\%$ HTTP error.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Contributions and Reflections on the Project}

This work contributes four durable scientific and systems-engineering deliverables:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Cryptographically Verifiable Multi-Domain Benchmark Suite:} Complete open Kaggle notebooks across 15 public five-class runs (\texttt{ce2a883e}, Appendix~\ref{app:kaggle_catalog}) and an isolated four-notebook, three-seed two-class archive (\texttt{SHWD\_V1/}) featuring SHA-256 deduplication and color-safe HardAug.
  \item \textbf{Unified-Protocol Reproduction of SOTA Helmet Detectors:} Full parameter-transfer audits and identical-split evaluations comparing \texttt{B1} (\texttt{YOLO11n-DCRCF}) and \texttt{B2} (\texttt{YOLOv8s-DWR-ASPP-NWD}) directly against \texttt{V2 A6} with verified domain-shift failure analysis.
  \item \textbf{Verified \texttt{RepConv} Deploy Fusion and Vectorized \texttt{BiFormer} Runtime:} Eliminated Python loop bottlenecks and quantitatively proved mathematical branch collapse across stride-2 \texttt{RepConv} blocks on physical GPU hardware.
  \item \textbf{Production-Ready AEGIS-CCTV Enterprise VMS v3.0:} Bridged the research-to-deployment gap with a flat enterprise VMS interface, 6-microservice fault-tolerant orchestration (Circuit Breaker + 4-table PostgreSQL 16 + Prometheus + Grafana), Dynamic Taxonomy Adapter, Class-Agnostic NMS, non-biometric Re-ID (\texttt{gdut\_reid.py}), Binary WebSocket streaming, Solo Focus rendering, and 139 automated regression tests.
\end{itemize}

Reflecting across the development cycle, the project reinforced a foundational engineering principle: \textbf{scientific credibility comes from disciplined measurement, clean baseline parity, and transparent negative reporting, not inflated claims}.

\section{Limitations and Future Work}

Four objective limitations bound the present work and directly guide follow-on development:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Non-Additive Full-Architecture Accuracy (\texttt{A6} vs. \texttt{A1}) and Single-Seed GDUT Runs:} Future five-class ablations will lock identical online augmentation across all branches, append \texttt{BiFormerBlockLite} residually \textit{after} \texttt{C2PSA}, and extend \texttt{GDUT-HWD} runs across 3--5 random seeds matching our \texttt{SHWD\_V1} protocol.
  \item \textbf{Bare-Head (\texttt{none}) Scale Bottleneck and Unfused Evaluation Script:} Improving bare-head test AP@50 beyond $84.6\%$ ($77.3\%$ recall) will explore tiled high-resolution inference ($960\text{--}1280$ px) on distant scaffolding ROIs and Active Learning seeded by \nolinkurl{Output/operator_feedback.jsonl}, alongside TensorRT FP16/INT8 export calibration.
  \item \textbf{Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Civilian Headgear Suppression:} Live webcam testing confirmed that models trained solely on industrial construction hardhats misclassify civilian pith helmets and motorcycle helmets; integrating targeted negative OOD background samples into Stage 2 training will strengthen field specificity.
  \item \textbf{Live Field Usability Validation (UAT/SUS):} Executing the finalized 10-item SUS protocol across $N \ge 10$ practicing site safety officers under multi-color industrial lighting will quantify field operational usability and refine zone-level alerting thresholds.
\end{enumerate}
