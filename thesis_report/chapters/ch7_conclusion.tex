\section{Summary of Findings}

This project delivered a reproducible safety helmet and color classification framework spanning an auxiliary two-class cross-domain study (\textbf{Proposed V1} on \texttt{SHWD} and zero-shot \texttt{HHW}) and a primary five-class benchmark (\textbf{Proposed V2} on RGB-pixel deduplicated \texttt{GDUT-HWD}), deployed inside the containerized \textbf{AEGIS-CCTV Edge VMS v3.0}. Core research and engineering milestones across \textbf{O1--O5} and \textbf{RQ1--RQ4} were completed with verified artifacts, while field UAT/SUS scoring, final slide synchronization, and the 3--5 min walkthrough video remain tracked as scheduled handoff items (Table~\ref{tab:app_review3_18}):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Deduplicated, Color-Safe Five-Class Data Pipeline:} RGB pixel SHA-256 hashing across all 3,174 raw \texttt{GDUT-HWD} images removed six exact cross-split duplicates from \texttt{trainval} while keeping all 1,587 official test images untouched (3,168 clean images; 18,849 boxes across \texttt{blue}, \texttt{white}, \texttt{yellow}, \texttt{red}, \texttt{none}). Locking \texttt{hsv\_h = 0} and \texttt{hsv\_s = 0} preserved four-color fidelity while pairing all 1,265 base scenes with deterministic degraded twins (2,530 Stage 2 images).
  \item \textbf{Unified-Protocol SOTA Baseline Comparison (\texttt{B1} and \texttt{B2}):} Across all 15 public Kaggle notebooks sharing digest \texttt{ce2a883e} (\ref{app:kaggle_catalog}, \texttt{75/75 MATCH}) and 48 matched Stage 2 hyperparameters, \textbf{V2 \texttt{A6 (YOLO11s)}} reaches \textbf{88.94\% mAP@50} and \textbf{58.08\% mAP@50--95} (\textbf{87.08\% macro F1}, \textbf{9,476,783} fused parameters, \textbf{15.207 ms} T4 FP32 median), outperforming \textbf{B1} (\texttt{YOLO11n-DCRCF}: \texttt{84.85\% / 54.77\%}, $+4.09 / +3.31$ pp; $26.22\%$ transfer ratio) and \textbf{B2} (\texttt{YOLOv8s-DWR-ASPP-NWD}: \texttt{87.51\% / 56.59\%}, $+1.43 / +1.50$ pp unrounded at $-41.8\%$ fewer parameters and $-32.1\%$ lower latency). \textbf{V2 \texttt{A6 (YOLOv8s)}} (\texttt{88.37\% / 57.37\%}) also exceeds both baselines.
  \item \textbf{Dual-Backbone Configurations (\texttt{A0}--\texttt{A6}) and Proposed V1 Cross-Domain Evidence:} Configuration \textbf{\texttt{A1}} (color-safe HardAug + \texttt{mixup = 0.1}) achieves the highest internal five-class accuracy (\texttt{YOLO11s}: $93.40\%$ val / $89.76\%$ test mAP@50, $61.69\%$ val / $59.08\%$ test mAP@50--95). Pre-finetune audits isolate a $-5.49$ pp drop when replacing \texttt{C2PSA} with \texttt{BiFormerBlockLite} on \texttt{YOLO11s}, whereas \texttt{YOLOv8s A6} preserves $91.88\%$ initialization and reaches $93.21\%$ validation precision. In three-seed \textbf{Proposed V1} (\texttt{SHWD\_V1/}), neck \texttt{RepConv} (\texttt{V1 A2}) cuts T4 FP16 latency to \textbf{13.55 $\pm$ 0.23 ms} ($73.8$ FPS, $p = 0.0386$ vs.\ \texttt{V1 A1}) with peak zero-shot \texttt{HHW} transfer (\textbf{95.78 $\pm$ 0.07\%} mAP@50, \textbf{56.48 $\pm$ 0.42\%} mAP@50--95, $4.7\times$ lower SD).
  \item \textbf{Edge Optimization and Containerized VMS Delivery:} On canonical \texttt{21866da3 $\to$ d422dd1d} weights on RTX 3050, vectorized \texttt{torch.gather}, verified \nolinkurl{RepConv.switch_to_deploy()} ($-300,992$ params before BN fold, $-10,800$ affine params via \texttt{model.fuse()}, $-5.1\%$ strict FP32 $20.206 \to 19.177$ ms, $-13.8\%$ AMP FP16 $11.321 \to 9.754$ ms, $4.20 \times 10^{-6}$ mean FP32 deviation), AMP FP16, and micro-batching ($N=4$) lift isolated detector speed from 82.3 FPS ($12.16$ ms) to \textbf{143.7 FPS} ($6.96$ ms/frame, $1.75\times$ speedup, $\Delta = 0$ boxes on a 12-frame sample parity check). Paired 1080p VMS + MobileNetV3 Re-ID runs at \textbf{65.7 FPS} ($15.22$ ms: $+3.07$ ms total Re-ID, $+0.19$ ms marginal over 3-Strip), and 1080p multi-stream passes the 25 FPS SLA for $N \in \{1, 2\}$ (\textbf{45.3} / \textbf{29.0 FPS/stream}). Packaged in six Docker microservices, \textbf{AEGIS-CCTV Edge VMS v3.0} passed \textbf{141/141} workspace CPU \texttt{pytest} cases (\textbf{84 passed, 1 skipped} in Clean-Room CI) and sustained \textbf{446.7--501.1 RPS} in-process and \textbf{225.2--260.8 RPS} across six containers at $0.0\%$ error.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Contributions and Limitations}

Four concrete outputs are delivered: (1) an open 15-notebook five-class Kaggle suite (\texttt{ce2a883e}) plus three-seed \texttt{SHWD\_V1/} archive with SHA-256 deduplication and color-safe HardAug; (2) unified-protocol reproductions of \texttt{B1} and \texttt{B2} with weight-transfer and bare-head audits; (3) vectorized \texttt{BiFormer} routing and FP32-verified \nolinkurl{RepConv.switch_to_deploy()} deployment on canonical \texttt{21866da3 $\to$ d422dd1d} weights; and (4) the containerized 6-microservice \textbf{AEGIS-CCTV Edge VMS v3.0} prototype with PostgreSQL 16, Prometheus/Grafana, non-biometric Re-ID, and 141 automated regression tests.

Five objective limitations guide future work:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=2.5pt]
  \item \textbf{Single-Seed GDUT Runs and \texttt{A1} vs. \texttt{A6} Confound:} Future ablations will lock identical online augmentation across all branches, append \texttt{BiFormerBlockLite} after \texttt{C2PSA} instead of replacing it, and run 3--5 random seeds matching \texttt{SHWD\_V1}.
  \item \textbf{Bare-Head (\texttt{none}) Scale Bottleneck:} Lifting bare-head test AP@50 beyond $84.6\%$ ($77.3\%$ recall) warrants tiled high-resolution inference ($960\text{--}1280$ px) on distant scaffolding ROIs, Active Learning via \nolinkurl{Output/operator_feedback.jsonl}, and TensorRT FP16/INT8 export.
  \item \textbf{Out-of-Distribution Civilian Headgear:} Live webcam checks showed misclassification of pith and motorcycle helmets; adding targeted negative OOD background samples into Stage 2 will improve specificity.
  \item \textbf{Laboratory Security Boundary:} Operator RBAC/JWT authentication and TLS ingress are not yet implemented, and optional Telegram/Zalo tokens send alert JPEGs outside the site LAN; production hardening will add JWT/TLS and strictly on-premises alerting.
  \item \textbf{Field Usability Scoring (UAT/SUS):} Executing the finalized 10-item SUS survey across $N \ge 10$ site safety officers will quantify operational usability and refine zone alerting thresholds.
\end{enumerate}
