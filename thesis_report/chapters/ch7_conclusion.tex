\section{Summary of Findings}

Capstone Project \textbf{FA26AI16} (\textit{Group GFA26AI17 -- NTD}) delivered a rigorously audited five-class safety helmet and helmet-color detection study on \textbf{GDUT-HWD} coupled with a containerized, real-time edge Video Management System (\textbf{AEGIS-CCTV VMS Safety Suite}). Across our experimental and engineering campaigns, all five project objectives (\textbf{O1--O5}) and four research questions (\textbf{RQ1--RQ4}) were systematically answered with verifiable artifacts:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Leakage-Free, Color-Safe Five-Class Data Pipeline:} Auditing all 3,174 raw Pascal VOC images of \texttt{GDUT-HWD} via RGB pixel SHA-256 hashing uncovered and purged six exact cross-split duplicates from \texttt{trainval} while keeping all 1,587 official test images intact (yielding 3,168 clean images and 18,849 bounding boxes across \texttt{blue}, \texttt{white}, \texttt{yellow}, \texttt{red}, and \texttt{none}). Disabling hue/saturation distortion (\texttt{hsv\_h = 0}, \texttt{hsv\_s = 0}) preserved four-color helmet fidelity while offline hard augmentation doubled Stage 2 training coverage to 2,530 images.
  \item \textbf{Forensic SOTA Baseline Audit (\texttt{B1} and \texttt{B2}):} Under matched five-class GDUT-HWD protocols and five-seed statistical replication (\texttt{seeds 3407--3411}), audited 1-to-1 across 16 public Kaggle notebooks, neither reproduced SOTA baseline outperformed its matched stock YOLO counterpart: \texttt{B1} (\texttt{YOLO11n-DCRCF}) scored $57.283 \pm 0.173\%$ validation mAP@50--95 ($54.37\%$ test) versus Stock \texttt{YOLO11n} (\texttt{C0}) at $58.812 \pm 0.082\%$ ($-1.529$ pp), driven by 73.78\% newly initialized neck weights and P3 bare-head score collapse (84/98 \texttt{none} misses). \texttt{B2} (\texttt{YOLOv8s-DWR-ASPP-NWD}, \texttt{V8F}) scored $60.781 \pm 0.353\%$ validation mAP@50--95 (exact 5-seed mean Precision $90.2447\% \approx 90.24 \pm 0.94\%$ under population SD \texttt{ddof=0}; $57.98\%$ test) versus Stock \texttt{YOLOv8s} (\texttt{V8S}) at $61.414 \pm 0.074\%$ ($-0.633$ pp) at 16.285M parameters and 41.23 GFLOPs.
  \item \textbf{Transparent Dual-Backbone Ablations (\texttt{A0--A6}):} All fourteen two-stage checkpoints outperformed \texttt{B1} decisively on official 1,587-image test accuracy, and our leading configurations surpassed \texttt{B2} at a fraction of its compute cost. Configuration \textbf{\texttt{A1}} (stock backbone fine-tuned with color-safe HardAug and online \texttt{mixup = 0.1}) achieved the highest overall accuracy on both backbones (\texttt{YOLO11s}: $93.40\%$ val / $89.8\%$ test mAP@50, $61.69\%$ val / $59.1\%$ test mAP@50--95; \texttt{YOLOv8s}: $93.14\%$ val / $88.7\%$ test mAP@50). Full architecture \textbf{\texttt{A6}} reached $92.50\%$ val / $88.9\%$ test mAP@50 on \texttt{YOLO11s} and $92.56\%$ val / $88.4\%$ test mAP@50 with peak $93.21\%$ validation precision on \texttt{YOLOv8s}, with pre-finetune initialization audits isolating the $-5.49$ pp \texttt{C2PSA} replacement penalty on \texttt{YOLO11s}.
  \item \textbf{Verified Edge Optimization and Industrial VMS Delivery:} Hardware profiling on RTX 3050 confirmed that vectorized \texttt{torch.gather} routing, verified \nolinkurl{RepConv.switch_to_deploy()} branch fusion ($-300,992$ params, $-6.3\%$ AMP FP16 latency, $6.28 \times 10^{-6}$ mean FP32 deviation), CUDA AMP FP16, and micro-batching ($N=4$) accelerate \texttt{Rep-YOLO11s-GDUT-A6} from 73.4 FPS ($13.62$ ms) to \textbf{118.2 FPS} ($8.46$ ms/frame, $1.61\times$ GPU speedup, $0$ box-count delta, mean confidence delta $0.00033$/$0.00032$), while within-frame paired MobileNetV3 Re-ID adds only $+0.16$ ms amortized overhead ($15.17 \to 15.33$ ms, 65.2 FPS). Packaged as both a 6-microservice Enterprise Docker stack (\texttt{nginx}, \texttt{api-gateway} + \texttt{HALF\_OPEN} Circuit Breaker, \texttt{ai-inference}, \texttt{postgres:16}, \texttt{prometheus} with Linux cgroup working-set RAM, 15-panel \texttt{grafana}) and a Single-Node Edge profile featuring Dynamic Taxonomy Adaptation, String-First color resolution, Confirm-Before-Mint non-biometric Re-ID with Two-Stage Gate, \texttt{C960 Ultra} Binary WebSocket, symmetrical 8-frame debouncing, Telegram/Zalo alerting, Image Audit, HITL feedback, and GitHub Actions CI/CD, the system passed \textbf{131/131 CPU \texttt{pytest}} cases (130 passed, 1 skipped in weightless CI stub mode) and sustained \textbf{446.7--501.1 RPS} in-process and \textbf{225.2--260.8 RPS} across 6 Docker containers at $0.0\%$ error.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Contributions and Reflections on the Project}

This capstone makes four durable technical and scientific contributions:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Cryptographically Verifiable GDUT-HWD Benchmark Protocol:} Complete open Kaggle notebooks (\texttt{nvthanh2004}, \texttt{tundng111}) featuring SHA-256 deduplication, color-safe HardAug, per-worker DDP graph manifests, and strict Val/Test separation.
  \item \textbf{Reproducible Multi-Seed Forensic Audit of Published Helmet Detectors:} Byte-verified baseline implementations (\texttt{B1}, \texttt{B2}) coupled with granular 98-FN bare-head error decomposition demonstrating why architectural bloat fails without preserved feature transfer.
  \item \textbf{Verified \texttt{RepConv} Deploy Fusion and Vectorized \texttt{BiFormer} Runtime:} Eliminated Python loop bottlenecks and quantitatively proved mathematical branch collapse across all six stride-2 \texttt{RepConv} blocks on physical edge GPU hardware.
  \item \textbf{Production-Ready AEGIS-CCTV Enterprise VMS v2.5:} Bridged the research-to-deployment gap with a flat Milestone/Siemens-inspired enterprise VMS interface, 6-microservice fault-tolerant orchestration (Circuit Breaker + PostgreSQL 16 + Prometheus + Grafana), Dynamic Taxonomy Adapter, String-First color decoding, Confirm-Before-Mint non-biometric Upper-Torso Re-ID (\texttt{gdut\_reid.py}) with Two-Stage Gate, \texttt{C960 Ultra} Binary WebSocket streaming, Solo Focus client-side vector rendering, stationary-worker protection, and 131 automated regression tests.
\end{itemize}

Reflecting across the semester, the project reinforced a foundational engineering principle: \textbf{scientific credibility comes from disciplined measurement and transparent reporting, not inflated claims}. Documenting exactly where \texttt{A6} trailed \texttt{A1} and why \texttt{B1}/\texttt{B2} trailed stock backbones yielded deeper engineering insight than any forced positive narrative could offer.

\section{Limitations and Future Work}

Four objective limitations bound the present work and directly define our post-capstone roadmap:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Non-Additive Full-Architecture Accuracy (\texttt{A6} vs. \texttt{A1}) and Augmentation Asymmetry:} Because \texttt{A1} used \texttt{mixup = 0.1} and \texttt{A6} replaced pretrained \texttt{C2PSA} on \texttt{YOLO11s}, future work will run a clean factorial ablation locking identical online augmentation across all branches and appending \texttt{BiFormerBlockLite} residually \textit{after} \texttt{C2PSA} (mirroring \texttt{YOLOv8s}'s function-preserving \texttt{SPPFWithBiFormer} pattern).
  \item \textbf{Single-Seed Execution for \texttt{A1--A6} and Unfused T4 Final-Test Timing:} Extending \texttt{A1--A6} to 3--5 random seeds will establish formal paired significance intervals, while integrating \nolinkurl{switch_to_deploy()} directly inside \nolinkurl{GDUT_final_test_evaluation.ipynb} plus INT8/FP16 TensorRT calibration will quantify fused cloud and embedded Jetson Orin Nano throughput end-to-end.
  \item \textbf{Bare-Head (\texttt{none}) Small-Object Bottleneck:} Improving bare-head test AP@50 beyond $84.6\%$ (recall $77.3\%$) will be pursued via tiled high-resolution inference ($960\text{--}1280$ px) on distant crane/scaffolding ROIs and hard-negative Active Learning driven by operator verdicts accumulated in \nolinkurl{Output/operator_feedback.jsonl}.
  \item \textbf{Field Usability Validation (UAT/SUS) at Live Construction Sites:} Following Review 3 defense, we will execute the prepared 10-item SUS questionnaire and four timed operational tasks across $N \ge 10$ practicing OHS officers on active Vietnamese construction sites to collect empirical usability scores and refine multi-camera zone dashboards.
\end{enumerate}
